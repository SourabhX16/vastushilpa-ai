# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Engineering judges and architects evaluating a hackathon prototype. They scan quickly and are skeptical of inflated claims.

## Product Purpose

Vastushilpa AI generates preliminary Vastu and Shilpa Shastra house plans, with checks against IS 456 and energy performance. Success means people can quickly inspect the plan and understand how its Vastu, energy, and structural assessments relate.

## Positioning

A single plan-generation prototype brings together traditional Vastu/Shilpa Shastra guidance, IS 456 structural checks, and energy-performance assessment. The three-way balance across Vastu, energy, and structure is central to how the product explains a plan.

## Operating Context

The product is demonstrated on a laptop or projector at a hackathon. Its audience needs to scan the result quickly and assess its claims.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Generates preliminary house-plan layouts from project inputs and presents Vastu, structural, and energy assessments.
- The existing prototype includes plan and report views and drawing export code.
- Plans are preliminary and not for construction.
- Estimated or unverified values must remain explicitly labeled. Do not present them as verified measurements or established engineering conclusions.
- The product is a hackathon prototype; do not imply certification, construction readiness, or validated performance without evidence.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the existing heritage theme: cream sandstone background, deep maroon, terracotta, antique gold, serif headings, and jaali, arch, and mandala ornament.
- Keep the existing palette and fonts. Reduce ornament only when it competes with data.
- Prioritize clarity and honesty over polish.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the working web prototype, including plan-generation, Vastu scoring, structural-check, energy-assessment, and export code. No independent validation or measured performance evidence was provided; do not invent it.

## Product Principles

- Make the balance across Vastu, energy, and structure the headline.
- Keep every estimated or unverified number labeled.
- Favor clarity and honest claims over polish.
